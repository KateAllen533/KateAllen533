Our Mission

To develop clinically validated AI solutions that empower healthcare professionals with faster, more accurate diagnostic and research tools, ultimately improving patient outcomes worldwide. We believe technology should serve clinicians, not burden them.

Our Vision

Our Story

From Research Lab to Global Impact

NovarisAI was founded in 2019 by a multidisciplinary team of AI researchers and clinical practitioners who saw a critical gap: powerful machine learning techniques existed, but they weren't reaching the clinicians who needed them most.

What began as a university research project in predictive diagnostics quickly evolved into a full platform when our early models demonstrated 94% accuracy in early diabetes detection. Today, we partner with over 200 healthcare organizations across 15 countries.

Our approach is different. We don't build AI in isolation. Every model is co-developed with practicing clinicians, validated through peer-reviewed research, and rigorously tested before deployment. This ensures our tools are not just technically impressive but genuinely useful in real clinical settings.

What Drives Us

Core Values

These principles guide every algorithm we design, every partnership we form, and every product we ship.

Clinical Integrity

Every model we deploy undergoes rigorous clinical validation. We hold ourselves to the same standards as pharmaceutical research because lives depend on accuracy.

Patient First

Technology is a means, not an end. Our success is measured by patient outcomes. Every design decision starts with the question: how does this help the patient?

Open Science

We publish our research, contribute to open-source projects, and believe that transparency is the foundation of trust in medical AI.

Data Privacy

Patient data is sacred. Our infrastructure exceeds HIPAA and GDPR requirements because privacy is a right, not a feature.

Global Access

World-class diagnostics shouldn't be limited to world-class hospitals. We design scalable solutions that work in resource-constrained environments.

Ethical AI

We actively test for bias in our models, ensure explainability in our algorithms, and maintain human oversight at every critical decision point.
